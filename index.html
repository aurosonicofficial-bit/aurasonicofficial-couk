What it brings your business

We talk about your business results, not about AI.

For most businesses the problem isn't a lack of technology; it's enquiries left unanswered, not knowing where the ad money goes, and the same work being redone by hand every day. Core takes on these burdens and leaves the decisions to you.

01

No enquiry goes unanswered

Everyone who reaches you via the form, WhatsApp, phone or ads is gathered in one register. Who to call back, and when, is in front of you every morning.

Today: messages on different phones, follow-ups in someone's memory.

02

Ad budgets are tied to measurement

You see which channel actually brings in customers or enrolments. Not likes and impressions; applications, meetings and outcomes are what count.

Today: the spend is known, the return is unclear.

03

Repetitive work comes off your team's shoulders

Daily lists, reminder messages, weekly summaries and content drafts are prepared by the agents. Your team doesn't prepare them; they read, correct and approve.

Today: skilled people spend hours on repetitive work.

04

Decisions reach your desk with evidence

Every report shows its source: which data, which date, prepared by whom. Results that weren't measured don't go into the report.

Today: the difference between "it's going well" and "we measured it, here's the number".

05

Knowledge stays with the organisation, not with individuals

Your rules, decisions and work history are on record. When an employee leaves or a piece of software changes, work picks up where it left off.

Today: the process leaves with the person who knows it.

06

You stay in control

Critical steps such as messages to customers, publishing and payments land in your approval box. Work you haven't approved isn't completed, and who approved what is on record.

Today: either you do everything yourself, or things run unchecked.

It's a fit if

  • You regularly receive applications, prospects or customer enquiries
  • Follow-ups, reminders and reporting are done by hand
  • You spend money on ads but can't measure the results
  • You have someone with authority to decide and approve

It may not be right yet if

  • You're looking for a "one-click, hands-free, does-everything" system
  • You can't assign someone to handle incoming enquiries
  • You don't want to share the data needed to measure results
  • You expect a guaranteed number of sales or enrolments

We don't promise outcomes. We agree the measure together before work begins, measure your real baseline in the first 30 days and evaluate everything after against those figures.

Frequently asked questions

Questions

What does "Super-Intelligent" mean? Is it a superhuman AI?

No. "Super-Intelligent Operating System" is a brand name: it describes many specialist agents working under one coordination and human supervision. We make no claim to superhuman general intelligence, unlimited autonomy or error-free decisions.

Is this a chatbot?

No. Each agent focuses on a single role (such as welcome, content or reporting) and works by your rules. The team is managed through an approval box and an audit log.

Will an agent message customers on our behalf?

It prepares the message; sending follows the approval flow you define. No bulk messages are sent without permission. Critical steps aren't completed without human approval.

Where is our data kept, and can anyone else see it?

Your accounts and enquiry records remain in your organisation's name. A separate space for each client comes into effect with our first client installations; no client data is processed until that separation is in place. Your data is never used for another client or for model training.

Who is responsible under data protection law?

You are the data controller for your own customer and applicant data; we act as the data processor under a written framework. Your legal adviser approves the privacy notice, consent and cookie texts; we set up the technical side.

How long does setup take?

A single-role setup reaches the test environment about two weeks after access is granted, and goes live once it passes acceptance tests. The clock starts from the handover of access, not from signing.

What do you need from us?

An authorised person and a deputy to handle enquiries; limited access to your current website, social media and ad accounts; and a little time each week for content approval. To measure results, confirmed sales or enrolments must be marked.

Do you guarantee results?

No; we don't guarantee numbers of sales or enrolments. What we promise is the method: the measure is written down at the start, the real baseline is measured in the first month, results are reported every month, and on day 90 the decision to continue is made on measured results.

What happens if an agent makes a mistake?

Because critical steps go through your approval, mistakes are caught before they reach a customer. Since every action is recorded, we find where the mistake originated, fix it and add a check for that same mistake.

Which AI models do you use? Are we tied to one provider?

Different models are used depending on the task. Identity, memory, permissions and work history are held in Core, not in the model; even if the model changes, your rules and records stay in place and the agent is re-verified.

Does it work with our existing software?
